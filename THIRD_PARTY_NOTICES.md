# Third-party notices

The frozen evidence includes materials from BrowserGym/AgentLab (ServiceNow, Apache-2.0), MiniWoB (OpenAI, Stanford and Farama Foundation, MIT), and RCI (Geunwoo Kim, MIT). The earlier app subset retains notices for GreenKart (Sivadass Navaneethan), SauceDemo (Sauce Labs) and TodoMVC (named upstream authors), all with MIT notices. App source/builds, screenshots and media are omitted from that subset; see its `scope.json`.

`third_party/notices.json` maps 7 unique retained notice texts to every located original path, including nested wheel members. These copies supplement the original notices retained in each evidence archive. The full Apache-2.0 text is included among them. Per-file notices and upstream exceptions continue to apply. This inventory does not assert that every asset shares one blanket license.

Dependencies installed by the reproduction commands have their own licenses; their code is not silently relicensed by the original-project license. Frozen wheels and upstream source snapshots remain unmodified. Changed experiment/adapter code and its scope are identified by each archived protocol and source record.
